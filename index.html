> What we do

Four ways we help your team ship better

Continuous Delivery

We design and build the Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery pipelines that let your team ship changes faster and with more confidence. For a government client, that meant a fully automated, zero-manual-step pipeline from commit to deployment — reducing risk and freeing the team to focus on features, not releases.

Containerisation

With extensive experience running production container and Kubernetes workloads, we help your business adopt containers the right way. For a financial services client, that meant migrating a live platform from image-based deployments to containers with zero-downtime releases — modernising the stack without disrupting the business.

Cost Optimisation

Use the cloud the way it was intended. We identify expensive and under-utilised services across your estate and help you lower cloud costs without compromising performance — from building cost-visibility dashboards out of raw billing data to right-sizing over-provisioned platforms for measurable savings.

Automation

Automate everything. We help your development teams become as autonomous as possible — from automated patching pipelines that keep environments secure, to self-service infrastructure provisioning, to AI-assisted tooling that accelerates legacy code migrations.
